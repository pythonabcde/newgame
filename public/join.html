Connection Sorting - How to Play

Game Rules:

  1. You control one gray circle in a shared world, but the game won't tell you which one.
  2. Use WASD or arrow keys to move.
  3. Press Spacebar to dash: a short speed burst in your last movement direction.
  4. Dashing has a cooldown. You must wait for the dash meter to refill before dashing again.
  5. Two colored zones move around the map edges. Your goal is to enter the correct zone with your hidden teammates.
  6. No talking or pointing at the screen, but you can use other non-verbal signals (within classroom rules) to coordinate.
